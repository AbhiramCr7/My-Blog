Why you install Burp’s CA certificate

Normal HTTPS shop.test shows a cert signed by
a public CA (Let’s Encrypt, DigiCert…).

Your browser already trusts those CAs.
Padlock is happy.
With Burp in the middle Burp cannot use the real private key
of shop.test — it does not have it.

So Burp makes a look-alike cert for
shop.test, signed by Burp’s own CA.

The first time you launch Burp it creates a unique CA on your machine. Trust that CA in the browser (or just use Burp’s browser, which already trusts it).
If you skip this, every HTTPS site will scream “Your connection is not private”.
