Backend path of one HTTPS request

1
CONNECT shop.test:443 — not TLS yet. Burp learns the hostname.
2
Browser ClientHello (SNI=shop.test). Burp mints host cert + host key, signs with CA key.
3
TLS handshake Browser↔Burp finishes. Session key A exists only on those two sides.
4
Burp ClientHello to the real server. Real cert. Handshake finishes. Session key B.
5
HTTP arrives encrypted with A. Burp decrypts with A. Plaintext sitting in memory.
6
Optional: mutate the plaintext (intercept). Then encrypt with B and send.
7
Response: decrypt with B, encrypt with A. Two rooms, two keys, same HTTP in the lobby.
