Step 1 — the CONNECT request

1
Browser wants https://shop.test. It does not speak TLS to Burp yet. It first asks Burp to open a pipe.
2
CONNECT shop.test:443 HTTP/1.1 — “please open a tunnel to that host.”
3
Burp answers HTTP/1.1 200 Connection Established.
4
Now the browser starts TLS. From its point of view the other end of that pipe is “the server”. In reality it is Burp.

No CONNECT is needed for plain HTTP. The browser just sends GET /… to 127.0.0.1:8080 and Burp forwards it.
